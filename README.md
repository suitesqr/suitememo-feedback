# SuiteMemo feedback

SuiteMemo is a Chrome extension that adds notes to NetSuite saved searches. This is where you can report a bug, ask a question or suggest a feature.

- **Report a bug or request a feature:** open an [issue](../../issues).
- **Ask a question or share an idea:** start a [discussion](../../discussions).
- **Anything private** (billing, your account, a security problem): email support@suitememo.com instead.

## Please don't post

This repo is public. Keep these out of issues, discussions and screenshots:

- The text of your notes
- Your NetSuite account number or URL
- Names, emails, saved search names or any data from your NetSuite account

If a screenshot helps, crop or blur the address bar and anything from your account first.

## Helpful details

- The SuiteMemo version, shown under Settings in the extension
- Your browser and its version
- Which page it happened on: the saved search list, the edit page or the results page
- What you expected, and what happened instead
